# Free Tools Hub

A privacy-first collection of free browser utilities.

## Current direction

The project is being expanded around durable 2026 interests identified in public research: practical AI use, learning and career skills, creator workflows, digital privacy, and short-form-friendly utility experiences.

The tools are designed to work locally in the browser without accounts or uploads.

## Revenue architecture

The site is intentionally built as a traffic asset rather than a guaranteed-income scheme. Future monetization can use clearly labeled advertising or legitimate affiliate partnerships where eligibility and platform rules allow. No age restrictions or identity checks should be bypassed; because the owner is a minor, any monetization account requiring an adult must be handled by a parent/guardian.

## Research signals

- Deloitte's 2026 Gen Z/Millennial survey reports widespread day-to-day AI use and strong interest in learning/career development.
- Google research published September 2026 reports teens using AI for homework, everyday questions, writing help and creative exploration.
- KT Nasmedia's 2026 internet-user research highlights short-form, creator influence and generative AI for information search.
- PwC's 2026 Gen Alpha survey highlights high smartphone use and early AI experimentation.

The project prioritizes useful tools over thin SEO pages or spam.
