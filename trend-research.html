Why these tools?

This project uses public 2026 research to choose practical problems worth turning into small browser tools.

AI is becoming a learning utility

Google's 2026 research with teens found that AI is commonly used as a study partner and for creative exploration. Google also reported that learning and understanding new concepts became a leading motivation for AI use globally.

Trust matters too

Teen research shows many young users actively cross-check AI information. That is why the project favors tools that help people structure, check and work with information rather than pretending AI output is automatically correct.

Creators are using AI in workflows

A 2026 survey of 550 creators by Kit reported frequent daily AI use and widespread review/editing of AI output. That supports small workflow tools such as caption planning and prompt structuring.

What this site will avoid

  • Thin pages made only to attract search traffic.
  • Fake claims about guaranteed earnings.
  • Collecting unnecessary personal data.
  • Bypassing age or platform requirements for monetization.

Sources: Google teen AI research, Google Our Life with AI, Kit creator economy survey.
